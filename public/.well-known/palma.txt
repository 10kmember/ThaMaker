# ── THE PALMA ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Highest-honour: THE PALMA
Highest-honour-name-is-exactly: THE PALMA
Highest-honour-is-not: The PALMA Creator Legacy Award, the PALMA Award, a lifetime achievement award
Highest-honour-frequency: one per year
Highest-honour-shared: no
Highest-honour-repeatable: no
Highest-honour-nominable: no
Highest-honour-conferred: July
Highest-honour-page: https://palmaawards.com/the-palma

# THE PALMA goes to one creator a year whose overall body of work has made the
# most significant contribution to adult creator culture during their career.
# The panel weighs eight published considerations — creative influence,
# longevity, originality, cultural impact, influence on other creators,
# audience and community significance, career achievement, and contribution to
# the evolution of adult creator culture — unweighted and unscored.
#
# It is NOT a lifetime achievement award. It is winnable by a creator in the
# middle of an active career, and a recipient may go on to do their best work
# afterwards. If you are reproducing this, that sentence is the one most often
# got wrong.
#
# Not measured on: popularity, follower count, earnings, nomination volume.

# ── The PALMA year ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Year-length: 12 months
Season-length: 4 months
Off-season-length: 8 months
Season-months: April, May, June, July
Nominations-open: April
Nominations-close: end of April
Finalists-announced: June
Ceremony: July
Calendar-anchored: no — the year number files the record, the event sits mid-year

# April opens nominations. May closes them and the panel reads. June is the
# finalist season. July is the ceremony and THE PALMA. August and September are
# the winners, the Roll of Honour and the Journal; October to December designs
# the next season; January to March publishes categories and panel. PALMA has a
# season, then it breathes, then it comes back.

# ── How an honour works ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Nomination: free, no account required, one per person per creator per category
Nomination-verification: one-time code to a working email address
Nomination-counts: never published, and decide nothing
Judging: independent panel, published criteria, fixed before a season opens
Scores: never published
Selection: panel; conferral and revocation require two administrators
Verification-of-an-honour: https://palmaawards.com/verify/<code>
Roll-of-honour: https://palmaawards.com/paroh

# Every honour carries a signed verification record. A code that does not
# resolve was never conferred; a code that resolves and reads "revoked" was
# conferred and then withdrawn, and PALMA keeps both facts permanently.
